Multi-layer Money Flow (A/B/E)

Tracks money across Schedule A contributions, Schedule B disbursements, and Schedule E independent expenditures.

This page shows how money flows through multiple layers of the federal campaign finance system. Schedule A tracks individual contributions to committees. Schedule B tracks how committees spend that money (disbursements). Schedule E tracks independent expenditures by outside groups for or against candidates.
