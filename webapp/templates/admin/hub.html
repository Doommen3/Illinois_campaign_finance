Admin Tools

Consolidated hub for data quality, entry, and management tools.

Data Quality & Reconciliation

Tools for auditing data integrity across local and federal sources.

ToolDescription
D2 Receipts Reconciliation Compare D2 form totals against itemized receipt sums per filing. Large differences may indicate missing data or reporting errors.
D2 Expenditures Reconciliation Compare D2 itemized expenditure totals against itemized sums, broken down by D2 parts (Transfers Out, Loans Made, Expenditures, Independent Expenditures).
Federal Receipt Audit Compare FEC-reported receipt totals against synced Schedule A subtotals. Flags discrepancies between official FEC totals and downloaded data.
Federal Disbursement Audit Compare FEC-reported disbursement totals against synced Schedule B subtotals. Identifies gaps in disbursement data coverage.

Data Entry & Review

Manual data entry and entity resolution review workflows.

ToolDescription
Manual Entry Queue Paper-filed reports requiring manual contribution data entry. Prioritized by filing date and report type.
Donor Merge Review Review algorithmically proposed donor entity merges. Approve or reject groupings of donor records that appear to represent the same person.

Data Management

Bulk data views and legacy tools.

ToolDescription
Candidate Finance ISBE bulk candidate-committee aggregate data with filtering, sorting, and CSV export. Use for validating state-level campaign finance totals.
Legacy Tools Historical scrape-era pages retained for QA, validation, and manual workflows. Includes committee, report, and donor views from the original data pipeline.
