About FollowTheMoneyIL.com

FollowTheMoneyIL.com is a civic-tech and data journalism platform for exploring campaign finance, lobbying, and related public records in Illinois.

What This Site Does

The project combines ETL pipelines, normalized database models, and web application tooling so people can search and investigate money-and-influence records with less manual cleanup.

Core records come from official filings and public datasets, including the Illinois State Board of Elections (ISBE) and the U.S. Federal Election Commission (FEC).

Where the Data Comes From and When It Arrives

All information on this site is based on reports filed by candidates and committees themselves with the Illinois State Board of Elections (state and local races) and the Federal Election Commission (federal races), and published in those agencies' public data files. Nothing here is estimated or collected from other sources; if a contribution or expenditure has not been filed, it does not appear.

Filing deadlines dictate when candidates and committees have to file all of their information. Between deadlines, any filing is each candidate's and committee's own decision: some committees report as money arrives, others report only when a deadline requires it. A candidate who shows little activity for a period may simply not have filed yet.

Dates shown with each record are the dates reported in the filing. The "Data Status" box on every page shows the latest state receipt date loaded and the last federal sync; filings received after that point appear with the next import.

Illinois Sunshine Attribution

This project uses ETL groundwork from Illinois Sunshine, built by DataMade for Reform for Illinois (formerly Illinois Campaign for Political Reform).

The original Illinois Sunshine project is available under the MIT License. FollowTheMoneyIL.com extends that base with additional data sources, analytics, and product features.

More on the original project: illinoissunshine.org/about/.
